# Customer demand: learning what customers will buy before the sale

A first-pass multivocal review, read on 2026-11-04. It asks how many interviews a segment needs, how far stated answers differ from actions, what a choice study needs, and what a study protocol must fix before the first question. It ends on a named baseline.

- It holds 40 sources: 31 formal and 9 grey, four of the grey below the threshold and serving as examples of practice only.
- Grey: 3 tier 1, 3 tier 2 and 3 tier 3. No trial of a structured protocol against an unstructured round was found.
- Formal: 19 read from the abstract (secondary) and 12 located only. Most figures are marked (secondary).
- Willingness to pay is held by [business-pricing](https://github.com/Tewf/business-pricing/blob/0d746beca6c5f2395e434ddbbc38c9d5a73d2cb2/evidence/willingness-to-pay/README.md) and linked, not repeated.

## Named baseline

- **B1, a protocol-fixed study with a choice arm, against an unstructured round of interviews.**
- The study is written in the [customer-study shape](../../shapes/customer-study.md); arm A is interviews, arm B a discrete choice experiment, run first on simulated data ([baseline](baseline.md)).
- The arms are compared on recovery of known coefficients, agreement of segments, new themes per interview and the effect size of intention on a paid action.
