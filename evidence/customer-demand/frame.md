# Frame: learning what customers will buy before the sale, and its names

Step 1 of the review, read on 2026-11-04. It states the problem, gives the field's names beside the practitioners', and says whether the repository's name matches.

**Shape:** [frame](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/c43f2770000a9af5c2aa3cbc6f997da7a7e13b34/shapes/frame.md)

## The problem in one sentence

How can a founder learn what customers will buy, and in which groups, from what they say and do before any sale, without the question, the sample or the incentive bending the answer?

## What the field calls it

| Level | The field's name | First met in |
|---|---|---|
| Estimating demand from choices | **discrete choice analysis**, from the **random utility model**, RUM | McFadden 1974 |
| The survey that elicits choices | **discrete choice experiment**, DCE, also **stated choice** or **choice-based conjoint**, CBC | Louviere 2010 |
| What a stated answer misses | **hypothetical bias**, and the **intention-behavior gap** | Haghani 2021a, Webb 2006 |
| Errors of asking | **survey measurement error**: **social desirability**, **satisficing**, **question order** effects | Tourangeau 2007, Krosnick 1991 |
| How many to ask, in interviews | **thematic saturation** and **sample size for saturation** | Guest 2006 |
| Behaviour instead of opinion | **field experiment** and **online controlled experiment**, A/B test | Kohavi 2009 |
| Groups whose demand differs | **market segmentation** and **preference heterogeneity**, by **latent class** or **mixed logit** | Kamakura 1996, McFadden 2000 |
| Fixing the study before it runs | **preregistration** and a **reporting checklist** | Nosek 2018, Tong 2007 |

## What practice calls it

- **Customer discovery** or customer development: talking to customers to test a hypothesis about a need (Blank 2013). The field's word is a qualitative interview study.
- **The Mom Test**: asking about past behaviour and not about the idea, so the answer cannot flatter (Fitzpatrick 2013). The field's word is an unbiased question, which survey methodology treats as one without a leading frame.
- **Validated learning** and **build-measure-learn**: a test of a business hypothesis with a minimum product (Ries 2026 Lean). The field's word is a field experiment.
- **Fake door**, **pre-order** and **waiting list**: a count of sign-ups before a product exists. The field's word is a revealed or incentive-aligned measure, held for price by business-pricing.
- **Opportunity solution tree** and **interview snapshot**: a coded record of each interview (Torres 2024). The field's word is thematic coding.

## Does the repository's name match?

- Partly. "Customer and demand" covers the practice and the demand estimate, and the field splits them: customer discovery is a qualitative and survey literature, demand estimation is discrete choice analysis.
- No rename is proposed. The two halves meet in one question, what a study must fix before the sale, and the repository keeps that question. The maintainer decides.
