# Against

The evidence against what the front page assumes, and the disagreements. Sources are in [review](review.md). None is deleted to make the subject look stronger.

**Shape:** [against](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/c43f2770000a9af5c2aa3cbc6f997da7a7e13b34/shapes/against.md)

## Against "a structured protocol beats an unstructured round of interviews"

- No source read tests the two against each other (review, Strand 7). Not found, in these searches.
- A scientific method taught to founders helped in a trial of 116 startups, which shows a method helps, not that a protocol shape does (Camuffo 2020, abstract, secondary). Measured.
- Critics of the lean start-up say it gives little guidance for generating hypotheses and that customer feedback limits learning (Bocken 2019, abstract, secondary, reporting Felin and colleagues). Convention.

## Against "a choice experiment estimates demand"

- Small design changes moved the results of many choice studies, and hypothetical bias was common (Rakotonarivo 2016, abstract, secondary; the figure is in review, Strand 0). Measured, descriptive.
- The reviews of hypothetical bias in stated choice exist because the problem is open (Haghani 2021a, Haghani 2021b, located only). Supported with caveat.
- An incentive helped prediction in two settings of cheap goods; a new durable good is not tested (Ding 2005, abstract, secondary). Measured.

## Against "a few interviews are enough"

- The saturation counts hold for homogeneous samples and narrow aims; multi-site and meaning-level work needed more (Hennink 2022, abstract, secondary). Measured, descriptive.
- Saturation as a concept is questioned for thematic analysis (Braun 2019, located only). Supported with caveat.
- The five-user figure comes from usability problems with L = 31%, not from customer needs (Nielsen 2000, article read). Measured, descriptive.

## Disagreements

| Point | One side | Other side |
|---|---|---|
| Does saturation fix a sample size? | 12 interviews, themes by 6 (Guest 2006, abstract, secondary) | 9 to 17, and more for outliers (Hennink 2022, abstract, secondary) |
| Is a stated answer a poor guide to action? | a medium to large change in intention gives a small to medium change in behaviour (Webb 2006, abstract, secondary) | choice results were consistent with other stated techniques (Rakotonarivo 2016, abstract, secondary) |
| Does the lean start-up work? | trained founders did better (Camuffo 2020, abstract, secondary) | its guidance on hypotheses is thin (Bocken 2019, abstract, secondary) |

- The splits may come from the goods, the populations and what is counted as saturation or as success.
